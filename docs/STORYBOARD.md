# The internet, built by me

Original direction, designed before implementation. October 11, 2026.

## Visual language

A luminous, open architectural atelier. Pearl #F7F9FF and porcelain #EDEFF8 form the room; deep ink #20243B typography; lavender #B9ACF4, periwinkle #8D9CE8 and cyan #70D8E5 identify the moving information. Silver #D9E1EE is the structural material. Geometry is rectilinear and sculptural, never a ring of miscellaneous floating icons. Soft shadows, long horizontal planes and a distant horizon give scale. No bloom or postprocessing is necessary.

Typography: locally bundled Space Grotesk for expressive, tightly set display text, Manrope for clear reading, and IBM Plex Mono for technical annotations. Small editorial markers, thin rules and large numbers provide an exhibition-like rhythm. The hero places ALI / BHATTI left, a large authentic cutout portrait centered, and FULL STACK / WEB DEVELOPER on the right, lower than the name. The portrait remains honest 2.5D.

## Continuous engine and pacing

Thirteen chapter poses share one full-viewport canvas, floor, light rig, 72 architectural beams and an information stream. Every beam has a stable identity and interpolates its position, rotation, scale and color between poses. This shared geometry is the physical transition chain. Scene-specific elements enter by unfolding from the shared structure, then fold into the next pose; scene replacement is never the primary transition. Scroll is the single clock for geometry, camera and HTML. There is a reading hold at each pose, followed by a smooth reversible transition. No wall-clock animation changes the story state. Pointer parallax is subtle and disabled for reduced motion.

On desktop, camera poses use a restrained changing azimuth and dolly around one continuous stage. On mobile, camera distance increases and structures compact into a narrower, deeper composition. Text moves to the top, installations remain central, and details use a compact bottom caption. Reduced motion locks the camera and snaps to stable chapter poses while preserving all content and navigation. No-WebGL uses a complete semantic reading view.

## Implementation contract

- `src/data/chapters.ts` is the editorial sequence; `src/scene/poses.ts` provides the corresponding numbered beam and camera poses.
- `src/engine/scroll.ts` derives all stage progress from native scroll, supports direct hashes, resizing and history.
- `src/scene/Portrait.ts` samples the real image and uses one particle cloud to connect its pixels to real code glyphs.
- `src/scene/installations.ts` creates meaningful chapter objects. The shared beam mesh persists throughout every transition.
- `src/components/Story.tsx` holds semantic chapter text and interaction counterparts. Inactive cinematic overlays are excluded from focus; the complete reading view remains available.
- `docs/QA.md` records observed testing, screenshots and limits; it must never substitute planned checks for actual results.
