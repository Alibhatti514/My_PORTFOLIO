## Synthesis
Navy #070B1D, indigo #10152F, blue #4776FF, violet #855BFF, lavender #B5A4FF, pearl #F5F6FF; cyan reserved for fine orbit traces. One observatory transforms through eight waypoints (six narrative scenes, with three project stops). Metallic rings introduced behind the portrait become skill orbits, frame the project displays, flatten into a credential orbit, then resolve behind the portrait again. Keep native scrolling and direct waypoint navigation. Skill selection and credential drag use the same live scene; important content and downloads stay in HTML.

These references provide art-direction and motion ideas, not evidence that their entire experiences are persistent 3D worlds. The portfolio architecture is purpose-built.

Interaction caveat: Michael's original image-link hover timed out because Framer kept the target outside the viewport. A separate visible navigation hover and mobile capture succeeded; see `michael-retry.json`. The timeout is retained in the raw interaction log rather than reported as a successful project hover.
